Dear AWS SES team,

Thank you for reviewing our production access request and for the four
questions below. Our answers:

1. Who you are / how you use SES

HustlePortal (https://www.hustleportal.online) is a South African business
platform for small service businesses. SES handles our transactional email:
account notifications, meeting/task confirmations, and lead-capture receipts.
A separate, clearly separated lane handles opt-in bulk mail (owner-initiated
campaigns to subscribers). Transactional volume today is low (tens of emails
per day) and grows with member signups; bulk campaigns start paused and are
sent manually by the business owner, not on schedules.

2. How you collect and verify recipient addresses

All addresses come from one of two sources. (a) Members register on the site
with their own email address and confirm ownership at signup; we only mail a
registered member about their own account. (b) Bulk mail goes only to
subscribers who explicitly opted in through a form that records the opt-in
timestamp and source. We do not purchase, scrape, or append lists. Every bulk
email carries a working one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058 List-Unsubscribe
header plus an in-app unsubscribe), honoured immediately against a permanent
suppression list (currently 575 entries from our legacy domain's history).

3. Bounce and complaint handling

Hard bounces suppress the address immediately and permanently; complaints
(via feedback loops) also suppress immediately and are reviewed within 24
hours. We monitor bounce rate and complaint rate on the SES dashboard before
and after every send, and we stop a campaign that approaches a 2% bounce or
0.1% complaint threshold. Suppression is enforced in application code at the
send boundary, not just at list level.

4. Sending rate and frequency

Transactional mail is event-driven and stays far below a few thousand per
day. Bulk sends are manual, throttled well below the SES default quota at
first (we plan to ramp from a few hundred per campaign), and never more than
one campaign per week per subscriber. We have no need for a quota increase
until volumes justify it and our metrics prove deliverability.

We keep sending domains DKIM-authenticated (easy-DKIM on the apex domain),
enforce SPF alignment, and route inbound owner mail through a single SES
receipt rule to a dedicated log address. Happy to provide any further detail,
headers of sample messages, or the unsubscribe/suppression code paths.

Thank you for your time.
